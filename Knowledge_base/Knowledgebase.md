# ZubeVision Tech Academy Knowledge Base

## Academy Name

ZubeVision Tech Academy is an academy focused on practical AI, data, automation, and full-stack AI engineering training.

The academy teaches students how to build real-world AI systems, not just theoretical concepts.

## Class Format

- Classes are online.
- Recordings are provided.
- Coding experience is not compulsory.
- Students will be guided step by step.
- Each program lasts 4 months.
- The first 3 months are for classes.
- The final 1 month is for capstone project development.
